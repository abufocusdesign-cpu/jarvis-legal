Jarvis

Jarvis is a personal voice and automation assistant built and used by one person, its owner. It is not a public product and is not offered to other users.

It helps the owner with everyday tasks on the owner's own Google account: finding and organising files in Google Drive, reading email, managing calendar events, creating Google Docs, and searching YouTube for video ideas.

Read how data is handled on the privacy policy.
